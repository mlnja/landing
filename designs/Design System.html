Voice & tone

11 · Language

Plain, confident, slightly understated. We're senior — we don't need to sell hard. Verbs over adjectives. Specific over abstract.

Do

"We ship ML systems that don't fall over."

"42ms p99, 99.98% uptime, drift 0.02."

"Took a notebook prototype to production in 6 weeks."

Don't

"Unlock unprecedented synergies with AI-powered transformations."

"Best-in-class enterprise-grade leveraged ML platform."

"🚀 Supercharge your data pipelines!"
